# Privacy Policy for Your Time Left (Android)

**Last updated: November 17, 2026**

This privacy policy applies to the Android version of the mobile application "Your Time Left" (also shown on your device as "Your Life Timer") (the "Application"), created by Robert Allan Bolista. The Application calculates and displays your age, the percentage of life lived, and the estimated time left based on the information you enter, and lets you keep a personal bucket list of goals.

**Short version: The Application does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.**

---

## No Collection of Personal Data

Your Time Left does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. The Application has no user accounts, does not use analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs, and does not request the Internet permission. All calculations are performed locally on your device.

## Information You Enter

The information you enter into the Application, such as your date of birth or current age, your chosen life expectancy, your bucket list goals and deadlines, and your light/dark appearance preference, is saved **only on your device** (using Android's local app storage) so the Application can remember it between launches. This information is never sent to us or to any server or third party.

You can remove this information at any time by deleting goals in the Application, clearing the Application's storage in your device settings, or uninstalling the Application.

## Android Backup

If you have enabled Android's built-in backup on your device, Android may include the Application's locally saved settings in your device backup to your own Google account, so they can be restored if you reinstall the Application or move to a new device. This backup is managed by Google under your account settings and is subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We have no access to it. You can turn backup off in your device settings.

## Permissions

The Application does not request any special device permissions. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

## Automatically Collected Information

The Application does not automatically collect any information from your device.

## No Data Sharing

Because the Application does not collect any data, there is no sharing of data with third parties.

## Children's Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Security

Because your information never leaves your device (apart from your own optional Android backup), it is protected by your device's own security, such as your screen lock.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

## Your Consent

By using the Application, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Application.
