# Privacy Policy

**Discover Toronto**
Last updated: November 22, 2026

## Overview

Discover Toronto is a travel discovery app for the Greater Toronto Area. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

**Short version: We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.**

---

## Information We Collect

Discover Toronto does **not** collect any personal information. Specifically:

- We do not collect your name, email address, or any account information
- We do not track your location
- We do not use analytics or crash reporting tools
- We do not use advertising SDKs or third-party tracking
- We do not store any data on external servers

Most content in the app (city guides, restaurants, creators, holidays) is bundled within the app itself. Some images, such as certain restaurant photos and creator avatars, are loaded over the internet from third-party image services (picsum.photos and DiceBear). These requests only download images; the app does not send any personal information with them. As with any web request, those services may receive standard technical information such as your device's IP address.

---

## Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves your light/dark appearance preference on your device. If Android backup is enabled, Android may include this setting in your own Google account backup, which is subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## Maps Integration

When you tap "Get Directions" on a location, the app opens **Google Maps** (or your default maps app) on your device. This is a standard Android system action. Any data Google Maps collects is subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## Children's Privacy

Discover Toronto does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The app contains no user accounts, forms, or data entry of any kind.

---

## Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
